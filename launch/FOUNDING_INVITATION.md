# Founding invitation

Prepared copy only. No recipients selected and nothing sent. Use for a small group Chris chooses after the live delivery path passes. Complimentary access does not enroll someone in marketing.

Subject: Would you try this?

I’ve been working on a course called All the Way Here, about bringing more attention to the people and life in front of us.

It starts with a short Attention Reset, then walks through practical ways to leave work at work, handle pressure, and make room for connection.

I’m inviting a small group to try it before I promote it more widely. I’d like to give you access in exchange for honest feedback. You don’t need to work through everything at once. Try one practice in a real week and tell me what helped, what felt awkward, and what you would leave out.

If you’re interested, reply and I’ll send your access link.

Chris

## Seven-day check-in

Which practice did you actually try? What happened when you used it? Was anything unclear or hard to fit into your week?

## Fourteen-day check-in

Have you returned to any of the practices? What would make this more useful? Would you recommend it to someone, and what would you tell them it helps with?

Ask separately before quoting any feedback publicly. Do not make a positive review a condition of access.
