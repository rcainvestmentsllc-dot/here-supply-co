# Iron Compass Launch Plan

## The decision

Iron Compass should be a small, useful resource business before it becomes a personality brand, a community, or a coaching company.

The clearest path is:

1. Help a husband and wife have one better weekly conversation for free.
2. Help a distracted man run one focused seventy-two-hour experiment for $29.
3. Help a man apply the full set of nine practices through Core.

Chris is part of the trust, but he is not positioned as a guru, clinician, or man with all the answers. The work stays in front. Chris is the real husband, father, and small-business owner who built it because he needed it too.

## Offer ladder and pricing

### The Sunday Board Meeting: free

Purpose: earn the first email address by solving a real, narrow problem.

Deliver:

- The revised printable guide immediately
- Five short emails over the following week
- A clear invitation to Focus only when attention is the next problem

### Focus Protocol: keep $29

The price is credible if the product includes the short orientation, revised visual Field Manual, platform setup instructions, five follow-up emails, and one simple tracking page. The manual by itself is not a strong $29 offer yet.

This price sits below a physical screen-time device such as Brick at $59 and close enough to annual software such as Freedom at $39.99 that a buyer can understand it as a small one-time experiment rather than a major course purchase.

### Iron Compass Core: launch at $99, then move to $149

Do not ask cold traffic for $249 yet. Core has good practices, but it does not yet have customer proof, a clean workbook, or enough outside validation to justify that price from an unknown founder.

Recommended pricing sequence:

- First ten paid buyers: $99 founding edition
- After ten real buyers and at least three useful customer interviews: $149
- Consider $249 only after the course rewrite is complete, the workbook is simplified, delivery is polished, and buyers have produced honest evidence that the practices were useful

Comparable official offers range widely. Dad University averages $29 per month for a large course library and community. Becoming Minimalist charged $89 for a twelve-week program with live support and a substantial audience. ManTalks lists a six-week self-study course at $127 and an eight-module relationship course at $247, backed by a long-running brand, extensive video, and testimonials. Those comparisons support a $99 to $149 starting range for a new, self-paced nine-lesson course without community or live coaching.

Give a Focus buyer a $29 credit toward Core for fourteen days. This is a real progression, not a fake discount.

### Private help: pilot later, not on the homepage now

If buyers ask for help applying the material, test a `Private Working Session` or `Implementation Review` at $125 for sixty minutes with a short written plan. Keep the scope to using Iron Compass practices. Do not call it therapy, marriage counseling, or broad life coaching. Do not add a subscription or package until individual sessions prove useful.

## Email system

### What exists now

- The public Sunday Board Meeting form loads and works.
- It has had twenty-three recorded visits and no completed signups so far.
- The five-email automation is complete but turned off.
- The automation currently uses `info@listingsinmotion.com` as both sender and reply address.
- It lives in the Listings in Motion MailerLite account, whose required company footer identifies Listings in Motion.
- The Iron Compass domain uses HostGator nameservers and HostGator mail. Its public SPF and DMARC records exist, but Iron Compass is not yet authenticated as a MailerLite sending domain.

### Correct structure

1. Create `chris@ironcompassinstitute.com` as a real HostGator mailbox.
2. Create a separate Iron Compass MailerLite account under Chris's existing MailerLite login. MailerLite recommends separate accounts for separate brands, and the free plan supports up to 250 active subscribers and 2,500 monthly emails.
3. Use `Chris at Iron Compass` as the sender name and `chris@ironcompassinstitute.com` as sender and reply address.
4. Add and authenticate `ironcompassinstitute.com` in the new MailerLite account using the exact DNS records MailerLite provides.
5. Recreate the Sunday Board group, embedded form, and five-email automation in the Iron Compass account.
6. Update the website with the new form code.
7. Test the entire path with a fresh address: form submission, double opt-in, guide access, first email, reply, unsubscribe, and mobile rendering.
8. Activate only after every sender, link, footer, and delay is verified.

The free account is enough at launch. Upgrade only when Iron Compass approaches 250 active subscribers or needs a paid-only feature.

## What makes the site findable

The technical foundation is already good. The site has canonical URLs, metadata, social images, a sitemap, robots rules, structured data, descriptive pages, and fast static delivery. The missing piece is not another redesign. It is useful, searchable material and measurement.

### Three resource pages to publish first

1. `A 15-Minute Weekly Marriage Meeting Agenda`
   - Answer the exact question a husband is searching.
   - Give the agenda on the page.
   - Offer the printable Sunday Board Meeting guide.

2. `How to Leave Work at Work Before You Walk Through the Door`
   - Teach the parked-car version of The Airlock Protocol.
   - Add alternatives for remote work, walking, and public transit.
   - Point to Focus or Core after delivering the useful practice.

3. `The Downward Glance: Put the Phone Away Before the Car Moves`
   - Use National Highway Traffic Safety Administration facts, not dramatic invented statistics.
   - Give one setup checklist: navigation set, important contacts allowed, phone out of hand, no response while moving.
   - Connect safe driving to the larger problem of automatic attention without turning tragedy into a sales pitch.

Google explicitly recommends people-first content with original experience, clear authorship, accurate sourcing, and titles using words people actually search. That favors a small number of strong field guides from Chris's real practices, not dozens of AI-written posts.

### Measurement before promotion

- Verify the domain in Google Search Console.
- Submit `https://ironcompassinstitute.com/sitemap.xml`.
- Add lightweight site analytics and track four events: Sunday guide view, confirmed signup, Focus checkout click, and Core checkout click.
- Record one weekly line: visitors, guide signups, Focus checkout clicks, Focus sales, Core sales.

Without those numbers, traffic activity can feel busy while teaching us nothing.

## The low-exposure traction engine

Chris does not need daily social video or a big public persona.

### Channel 1: search

Publish one excellent resource every two weeks from material that already exists in the course. Every page should solve a complete small problem, show who wrote it, cite real sources where claims matter, and lead to only one relevant next step.

### Channel 2: borrowed trust

Build a one-page professional resource note for:

- marriage and family therapists
- church men's groups and pastors
- fatherhood organizations
- business coaches and small-business peer groups
- local organizations concerned with distracted driving

The request is not `endorse my course`. It is: `I made a free fifteen-minute weekly meeting guide. Would you be willing to tell me what is useful, what is risky, and whether you would ever share it?`

Five thoughtful reviews from qualified or experienced people are more valuable than a fake accreditation badge. If someone agrees to be named, quote only their exact words and describe the relationship accurately.

### Channel 3: referral built into the free guide

After the fifth email, ask one simple question: `Did you use the meeting guide?` If yes, invite the reader to forward the public link to one husband who could use it. Do not attach the PDF or create a complicated referral program.

### Channel 4: small paid search after the funnel works

Do not buy broad social traffic first. Once the signup and purchase path is measured, test a small Google Search campaign at $5 to $10 per day on narrow intent such as `weekly marriage meeting agenda`, `how to be more present with family`, and `leave work stress at work`. Stop any keyword that produces clicks without confirmed signups.

## Thirty-day operating plan

### Week 1

- Create and verify the Iron Compass mailbox
- Separate Iron Compass into its own MailerLite account
- Rebuild and test the five-email path
- Change Core's public price to $99 founding edition after approval
- Add measurement and Search Console

### Week 2

- Rewrite Focus Manual claims and instructions
- Publish the weekly marriage meeting resource page
- Ask three trusted but independent readers for criticism, not praise

### Week 3

- Rewrite Core lessons 1.1 through 1.3
- Publish the work-to-home transition resource page
- Invite the first small group of cold visitors through search and partner sharing

### Week 4

- Rewrite Core lessons 2.1 through 3.3
- Simplify the workbook
- Publish the distracted-driving resource
- Review the first conversion numbers and five real user conversations

## Do not build yet

- A community
- A membership
- A mobile app
- A complicated course portal
- A daily content machine
- Multiple coaching packages
- Paid ads before conversion tracking works
- Accreditation language or borrowed authority
- Artificial scarcity, countdown timers, or inflated value stacks
