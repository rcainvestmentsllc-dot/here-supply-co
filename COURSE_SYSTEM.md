# Iron Compass Course System

## The promise

Iron Compass is a field manual for coming back to your life. It helps a man choose where his attention goes, enter important rooms on purpose, carry pressure more cleanly, protect his relationships, and renew the parts of life work cannot replace.

The tone is practical, direct, warm, and unfinished in the honest sense. Chris is not presented as a guru, therapist, doctor, or accredited authority. He is a husband, father, small-business owner, and builder of useful practices who cites stronger sources when a claim reaches beyond lived experience.

## The native delivery path

1. The public offer page explains the problem, outcome, exact contents, price, refund window, and next step.
2. MailerLite checkout collects payment and adds the buyer to the correct product group.
3. The successful purchase opens a private welcome page on ironcompassinstitute.com.
4. The buyer uses the course, lesson pages, videos, field assignments, and workbook on the Iron Compass site.
5. Progress is stored in the buyer's browser during the founding release.
6. Buyer email support comes from chris@ironcompassinstitute.com. Automated delivery and follow-up should remain off until the Iron Compass sending domain is authenticated in MailerLite.

The founding release uses hard-to-guess private URLs. It does not yet provide individual accounts, cross-device progress, or share-proof access. A purchase-aware email login becomes the next infrastructure step after the offer shows real demand.

## The reusable course blueprint

Every paid course should include:

1. A public offer page
2. A clear buyer and problem
3. One honest outcome
4. A short welcome and orientation
5. Three to five modules
6. A lesson page for every practice
7. One field assignment per lesson
8. Words a buyer can actually use
9. A mistake or limitation to watch for
10. A cited evidence note when research informs the teaching
11. A workbook or field notes page
12. A final integration plan
13. A checkout product, private welcome route, and buyer group
14. A support and refund path

## The Iron Compass lesson anatomy

Each lesson answers these questions in order:

- The room: Where does this problem show up in real life?
- The principle: What is the durable idea underneath it?
- The practice: What does the buyer do?
- The fit: How does it bend for different jobs, families, bodies, ages, and seasons?
- The script: What can he actually say?
- The warning: How could this practice become avoidance, control, shame, or another system to manage?
- The field assignment: What will he try before moving on?
- The evidence note: What is supported, what is an Iron Compass interpretation, and where can he read further?
- The reflection: What did he notice?
- Chris's field note: Why this matters to a real man still doing the work.

## Originality guardrail

Iron Compass can learn from durable ideas in leadership, habit formation, relationship research, behavior change, faith, and personal development. It must not copy another teacher's branded framework, signature language, worksheets, diagrams, stories, or sequence.

The original Iron Compass practice cycle is:

- Choose
- Enter
- Protect
- Listen
- Renew

Core is organized as:

- Return
- Lead
- Keep

## Brand world

The visual direction is sun-faded coastal modernism. It blends Puerto Rico, old California surf culture, Hawaii, mountain and trail life, and restrained mid-century design.

Use:

- warm paper and sand
- sea-glass teal
- faded coral and rust
- sun gold
- sage and kelp green
- natural photography with real movement and unforced expressions
- confident serif headlines with a clean humanist sans serif
- horizon lines, simple sun discs, and restrained wave geometry
- generous spacing and strong modular grids

Avoid:

- dark fantasy masculinity
- nautical-compass clichés
- fake credentials or authority poses
- heavy black-and-gold guru branding
- chaotic overlapping cards
- generic stock men staring at mountains
- overly polished life-coach language
- decorative waves that resemble clip art or an eye

## Expansion map

Potential future courses should be tested as focused problems before becoming large programs:

- Screen Reset: break the reflex and rebuild phone boundaries
- The Marriage Room: listen, repair, decide, and reconnect
- Fatherhood in the Room: attention, play, limits, and growing with the child
- The Courage Track: uncertainty, risk, identity, and action
- Faith and Practice: a clearly labeled faith-centered track, not quietly inserted into every buyer's course
- Work Without Disappearing: ambition, boundaries, leadership, and coming home

Each starts as one strong resource or live workshop. Demand, completion, and buyer questions determine whether it becomes a mini-course or a new Core track.
