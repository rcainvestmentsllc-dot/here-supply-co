# Here Supply Co. rebuild brief

## Decision

GPT Sites is the only editable website source. MailerLite remains the payment and delivery system. Claude's separate login or magic-link layer is not part of the launch.

The founding version should be deliberately simple:

1. A visitor gets the Sunday Board Meeting free, without an email gate.
2. They can buy **All the Way Here** for $99 through the existing MailerLite checkout.
3. MailerLite sends the order confirmation and delivers the private course link.
4. The buyer can save that link and return to the course. A future account system is a later decision, only after real demand proves it is useful.

## What is verified now

- `heresupplyco.com` is publicly served from the Sites project.
- The current product is active in MailerLite as **All the Way Here**, priced at **$99**.
- Stripe is connected in MailerLite.
- The current checkout URL is `https://checkout.mailerlite.com/checkout/34347`.
- Delivery is configured to send the buyer to `https://heresupplyco.com/access/core-4m8r2p`.
- The current product confirmation and product email still use old Iron Compass language and a Listings in Motion sender. These must be replaced before paid traffic is sent.
- The public site contains newer work that is not represented by the current editable Sites source. Publishing that source without reconciliation could roll the live site backward.

## Brand direction

Use both parts of the approved direction in a disciplined way:

- The warm coastal wave logo is the recognizable brand mark, used in the site header, checkout, printables, and a few high-impact moments.
- The rest of the experience uses clean editorial type, large readable text, real photography, ocean navy, blue, coral, and warm cream.
- The voice is for two people, including couples with or without children. It speaks plainly about divided attention, pressure, missed moments, repair, and a better shared week.
- Retire the compass, tan/brown, mustard, old Iron Compass names, and generic self-improvement language from public pages and buyer emails.

## Product architecture

### Free: Sunday Board Meeting

One printable, one complete watch-together video, and a clear next step. It should give people a quick win and show the teaching style. No forced signup.

### Paid: All the Way Here

One $99 founding-edition course. It includes the course, printable workbook, field cards, and family tools. There is no separate public Focus product, separate $29 offer, or competing course path.

### Founding Circle

An invite-only feedback group of 12 to 15 couples. They receive free full course access and are asked for honest feedback after trying it. Testimonial permission is requested separately, after feedback, and never as the price of free access.

## Launch sequence

1. Reconcile the GPT Sites source with the current public site before publishing.
2. Build a shared brand system and apply it to the public routes, buyer course, and printable materials.
3. Replace all public Iron Compass language, including product confirmation and buyer email.
4. Set the product sender to `Chris at Here Supply Co.` with `hello@heresupplyco.com` only after the mailbox can send and receive successfully.
5. Update checkout branding, description, product image, confirmation page, and delivery email as one unit.
6. Run a real buyer-path test with a controlled test purchase or a one-use full-discount checkout, then verify payment confirmation, buyer email, link, course, video controls, and printables.
7. Invite the Founding Circle. Use what people say to revise the sales page and course before wider promotion.

## What must not happen

- Do not publish the stale editable source over the newer public site.
- Do not turn on the old Sunday Board automation. It has zero subscribers and old sender details.
- Do not use a Listings in Motion address or Iron Compass name for Here Supply buyers.
- Do not promise a login or locked course access until there is a real, tested entitlement system.
- Do not launch more offers or products before the one paid course completes a clean buyer test.
